# Northstar Trail

Northstar Trail makes lightweight, repairable trail-running equipment for runners who spend more time outdoors than online.

## Audience

Design-aware trail runners, hiking clubs, and outdoor retailers.

Technical, resilient, quiet, and modern. Avoid glossy luxury and loud performance cliches.
