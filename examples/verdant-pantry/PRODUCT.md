# Verdant Pantry

## Company

Verdant Pantry sells low-waste pantry staples, botanical seasonings, and refillable kitchen goods.

## Audience

Urban home cooks who value sustainability without wanting a rustic craft-market aesthetic.

## Personality

Fresh, intelligent, restrained, and tactile.
