# Brand analysis: Razor Eater

**Purpose:** Indo-Chinese fast-moving food and takeaway brand focused on bold, quick, craveable urban meals.

**Visual direction:** The supplied black geometric wordmark and pale blush field are preserved, supported by a chili-red and warm-orange packaging system appropriate to fast food.

## Applications

- Noodle takeout carton
- Takeaway paper bag
- Sauce and seasoning pouch
- QSR storefront
- Campaign billboard
- Staff T-shirt
