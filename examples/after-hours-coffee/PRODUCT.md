# After Hours Coffee

## Company

After Hours Coffee is a late-night neighborhood coffee bar and small-batch roaster built for students, musicians, and night-shift workers.

## Audience

Creative young adults who want an independent cafe with more edge than a daytime lifestyle brand.

## Personality

Warm, nocturnal, irreverent, and social.
