# Premium identity direction: AI application

The six-arm mark is treated as a physical identity system: printed into technical fabric, fabricated as raised signage, backlit in reception, and applied as frosted vinyl to smoked glass.

## Palette and materials

- Electric lime for energy and machine intelligence
- Deep ink and cobalt for technical depth
- Graphite, ribbed metal, slate, concrete, glass, and woven textile
- Controlled daylight and architectural lighting instead of flat colored rectangles
